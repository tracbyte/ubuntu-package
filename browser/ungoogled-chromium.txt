install:
========
flatpak install flathub com.github.Eloston.UngoogledChromium -y

upgrade:
========
flatpak update com.github.Eloston.UngoogledChromium

uninstall:
==========
flatpak uninstall --delete-data com.github.Eloston.UngoogledChromium -y

clean cache:
============
flatpak cleanup

remove profiles:
================
rm -rf ~/.var/app/com.github.Eloston.UngoogledChromium

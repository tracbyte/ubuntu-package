install:
========
curl -fsSL https://ollama.com/install.sh | sh

upgrade:
========
curl -fsSL https://ollama.com/install.sh | sh

uninstall:
==========
sudo systemctl stop ollama
sudo rm -rf /usr/local/bin/ollama /usr/share/ollama

clean cache:
============
rm -rf ~/.ollama/history

remove profiles:
================
rm -rf ~/.ollama
